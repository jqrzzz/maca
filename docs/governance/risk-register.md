# Risk register — starter

_Internal working document for the founder (and the future board — reviewing
this becomes a standing board agenda item). Grantmakers increasingly ask to see
one. Honest by design: likelihoods are the founder's call to confirm, and the
mitigations marked ✅ exist today while ☐ items are still to do. Review
quarterly; update `Last reviewed` when you do._

## How to use this

- Review quarterly (or after any incident). Re-score likelihood/impact, tick
  mitigations that now exist, add new risks.
- When the board forms, this becomes a standing agenda item — it's also exactly
  what grant due-diligence asks for.
- Keep it honest: a register that says "all mitigated" is a register no one
  believes.
