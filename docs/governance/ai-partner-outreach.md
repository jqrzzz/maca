# AI-partner outreach pack

_The concrete next move now that the public AI story is live ([`/ai`](../../app/ai/page.tsx)) and the [Responsible AI Policy](../../content/legal/responsibleAi.ts) is published: approach mission-aligned AI labs and their social-impact / access programs so PRASM gets model access, compute, technical help, or funding for the engine room. This pack is **ready-to-send drafts plus the kit**, grounded only in verified facts, with `[fill in]` slots for what only the founder knows. Review, fill, send. Nothing here overclaims: PRASM is presented exactly as what it is, an emerging, founder-led foundation that already uses AI responsibly in its back office._

_Method note: program names below change often. Treat each as a **candidate to verify** (current name, eligibility, and how to apply) before sending. Confirm there is a genuine fit rather than mass-emailing._

## Why a lab would say yes

Lead with the thing that de-risks it for them: **responsibility**. Most "AI for good" pitches are a liability worry. Ours is the opposite, and we can prove it:

- A published **Responsible AI Policy** aligned to the OECD AI Principles, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, and the UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI.
- A real, hard, **values-aligned deployment**: refugees, off-grid, multilingual, with health, education, and identity needs.
- **People stay the face**; AI is the engine room. No stunt, no vulnerable-people-as-marketing.
- We are **already AI-native in the back office** (field-to-story, grants, donor stewardship, clinical reference), so this is augmentation that works, not a science project.

For each, note: do they offer **credits, model access, technical help, or funding**? Is there an **application** or a **warm intro** path? What is their **eligibility** bar (registered nonprofit, fiscal sponsor, region)?

## The partnership summary (attach or paste when they ask)

> **PRASM, dignity, identity, and care for the stateless, with AI in the engine room.**
>
> PRASM supports Kayan refugees from Myanmar who fled war and now live, largely undocumented and off-grid, in Mae Hong Son, Thailand. Without Thai ID, families are charged full foreigner rates at hospitals, children are barred from public school, and many people have no identity document at all.
>
> Founded by a medical doctor, PRASM covers urgent treatment and the journey to reach it, supports learning and the village's solar, water, and food systems, and builds a hand-written medical record for each patient, the seed of a future identity and records program.
>
> **How AI helps, responsibly.** A tiny team is amplified by AI in the back office: turning field input into honest updates and grant drafts, keeping clean records, and (on the roadmap) translation, clinical reference, and a privacy-preserving identity and case registry. People are always the face. Sensitive data never goes to a general-purpose model. A human approves everything public, clinical, or personal. See our Responsible AI Policy.
>
> **What we are looking for:** [model access / compute credits / privacy-preserving or on-device options for sensitive work / technical mentorship / funding to move from authoring tools to a secure records platform].
>
> **What we offer:** a real, high-integrity deployment, a documented responsible-AI framework already in practice, a people-first story told with dignity, and an openness to share what works.

## Questions to ask THEM

1. Do you offer **credits, model access, technical collaboration, or funding**, and which fits an early-stage, Thailand-based, foreign-led nonprofit?
2. What is your **eligibility** bar (registered nonprofit vs fiscal sponsor vs project), and does a US fiscal sponsor satisfy it?
3. For our **Tier-2 sensitive data** (identities, medical records), do you offer **privacy-preserving, on-device, or zero-retention** options?
4. What **data-use and retention** terms apply to prompts and outputs on your platform?
5. Is there a **responsible-AI or safety review** we should expect, and what do you need from us for it?
6. What **support** comes with access (engineering time, office hours, a named contact)?
