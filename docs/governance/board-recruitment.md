# Recruiting PRASM's first board

A practical one-pager for the founder. A governing board is the single biggest
credibility step PRASM can take — most serious funders look for one before they
give. Here's how to start.

## Why a board (now)

- It's what grantmakers, partner NGOs, and larger donors expect to see.
- It shares the weight of decisions, money, and safeguarding beyond one person.
- It's usually required to register as a Thai foundation (and by most fiscal
  sponsors).

## What "good" looks like at our stage

- **Size:** start with 3–5 members. Thai foundations have a minimum board size —
  confirm the exact number with a Thai lawyer (part of the entity decision).
- **Independence:** at least a couple of members who are not the founder and not
  family, so decisions aren't all one person's.
- **Commitment:** a few hours a quarter — meet, review finances and safeguarding,
  approve the big decisions, keep minutes.

## Skills to aim for (not all in one person)

- **Thai NGO / legal** — someone who knows Thai foundation law and compliance.
- **Finance / bookkeeping** — a treasurer who can keep and review the accounts.
- **Safeguarding / child protection** — given who we serve.
- **Community / Kayan voice** — someone trusted by, or from, the community, so
  decisions aren't made only by outsiders.
- **Fundraising / communications** — helpful, not essential at first.

## Where to look

- Trusted contacts in the Mae Hong Son / border NGO community.
- Thai nonprofit networks and the lawyers who set up foundations.
- Diaspora or existing supporters with relevant skills who already believe in
  the work.
- A respected community elder or representative for the community seat.

## A short outreach note you can adapt

> PRASM is an emerging initiative supporting Kayan refugee families in northern
> Thailand — covering medical care, building records that help people prove they
> exist, and backing a self-reliant off-grid village. We're forming our first
> governing board to oversee strategy, finances, and safeguarding as we register
> and grow. It's a few hours a quarter. Given your background in [X], would you
> consider joining — or pointing us to someone who might?

## Once you have names

- Confirm each person is willing and has no disqualifying conflict (see the
  Conflict of Interest policy).
- Hold a first meeting, record minutes, and adopt the policy suite at
  `/governance`.
- Update the Governance page and the Transparency registration details with the
  board — tell me and I'll put the names live.

_This is internal guidance, not legal advice. Confirm board size and duties with
a Thai lawyer as part of the entity decision._
