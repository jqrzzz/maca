# Security policy

PRASM runs a small, mostly static website (no backend; donations are handled by
third-party providers under their own security). We still welcome reports of
security or privacy issues in the site or this codebase.

## Reporting

Please reach us through the **contact page on the PRASM website**, and we'll
respond as quickly as we can. A machine-readable contact is published at
`/.well-known/security.txt`.

If a report touches the **safety or privacy of the community we serve** — for
example, anything that could identify or locate people — say so, and we'll treat
it as urgent.

## Please

- Give us a reasonable chance to fix an issue before disclosing it publicly.
- Don't access, change, or store data that isn't yours while testing.
- Don't run denial-of-service or spam tests against the site.

## Scope

In scope: this website and this repository. Out of scope: the third-party
donation providers (Stripe, PayPal, Patreon) and crypto networks, which run their
own security and disclosure programs.
